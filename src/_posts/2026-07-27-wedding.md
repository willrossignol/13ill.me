I feel so grateful for the past weekend, and I know this feeling won't stay with me for too long, so I want to tell you all that I'm feeling.

This past weekend, I was fortunate enough to watch one of my best friends marry his best friend. As a groonsmen, I was let in on all of the nuances that come with such a big event. The love, the laughter, the uncertainty, the anxiety, the tears. It was an honor to witness this beautitul moment.

Whilst participating in this ceremony, I got to know the newlyweds' families and friends on a deeper level, making connections with people through shared love. There arent't many things more beautiful.

Celebrating love makes for unforgettable memories. And I am so so so happy to have had the chance to celebrate this love with some of my dearest friends as well as with some of their families, whom I can now call my friends.

My heart is full.

Félicitations à Elisabeth & Gabriel Dionne.

I wish you a happy and healthy marriage.

Cheers to many more good memories together.

With love <3
Will Rossignol
